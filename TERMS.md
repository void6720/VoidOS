## 1. What VoidOS is

VoidOS is an experimental operating system project built for learning and
testing: hand-written bootloader and kernel sources, plus VoidOS Linux —
an Alpine-based distribution with a custom-compiled kernel, the XFCE
desktop, and the vpm package manager. Builds are test builds, not a
finished product, and they may not boot on every machine.
